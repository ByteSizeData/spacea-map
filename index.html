🛄 Baggage & boards — terminal cheat card
Same rules at every terminal — the board bag is the same fight everywhere.
  • Allowance (big jets — C-17 / C-5 / KC-10 / -135 / Patriot Express): 2 checked bags each, 70 lb & 62 linear inches. As a couple = 4 pieces / 140 lb + carry-ons.
  • Board bags are oversized by definition. Each passenger gets ONE oversized exception item (golf clubs · surfboards · snow skis · bikes · fishing gear · instruments) — so both your board bags fly. ✅
  • “Kiteboard” isn't on the printed list — “surfboard / snow skis / golf clubs” is. If a by-the-book terminal balks, name the listed twin (a 165 cm Mystic ≈ 90+ linear in.).
  • WEIGHT is the real trap. Keep each board bag under 70 lb / 31.7 kg or it flips to counting as two pieces. Spread pump, harness, bar & boots into your regular checked bag.
  • Big jets ONLY. Small admin birds (C-12 / C-21 / UC-35) = 30 lb total incl. carry-on, and the oversized-exception list applies to large aircraft only → a board bag can't fly on those. Don't roll-call them for a gear trip.
  • Still space-available. On a packed cargo mission, oversized bags bump first — keep a refundable board-ship backup.
🛩️ Odds of hitting a board-killing small plane ≈ 2–5% — near-zero on the ocean crossings (all big-jet Patriot Express / C-17 routes). If only a small bird is going your way, just wait for the next big-jet roll-call.
🏂 Bring a splitboard
Splits into two flat halves — resort + backcountry + snowkite touring in one bag. One-line rule: splitboard ≤ 162 cm, bag ≤ 175 cm & ≤ 23 kg (50 lb), boots in your carry-on — clean on every aircraft and every onward airline.
  • Bag weight — hard ceiling: ≤ 70 lb / 31.7 kg (over = counts as 2 pieces on Space-A).
  • Bag weight — smart target: ≤ 50 lb / 23 kg (free checked on Ryanair / Southwest / JAL onward hops).
  • Bag length: ≤ 75 in / 190 cm · linear (L+W+H): ≤ 80 in / 203 cm — zero arguments anywhere.
  • Board deck: 61–64 in / 154–162 cm — the splitboard all-mountain + touring sweet spot.
  • Carry-on: ≤ 45 in linear / 114 cm — boots, helmet & skins ride here to keep the board bag light.
